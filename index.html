My name is Davide Coluccia. I am a Lecturer (British English for Assistant Professor) of Economics at the University of Bristol.

My research agenda intersects themes in the Economics of Innovation, Political Economy, and Economic History. I study how technology diffuses across and within countries to generate inclusive economic growth, focusing in particular on international migrations, labor markets, and culture.
